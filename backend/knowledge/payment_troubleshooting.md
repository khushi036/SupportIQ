# Payment Troubleshooting FAQ (v1.2)

## Failed Payment Debits
If money was deducted from your bank account or UPI wallet during a failed checkout:
- Bank systems usually auto-reverse pending transactions within 24 to 48 hours.
- If your funds are not refunded after 48 hours, contact support with your Payment Transaction Reference ID.

## Accepted Payment Methods
We support Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking, UPI (Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay), and Cash on Delivery (COD) up to ₹10,000.

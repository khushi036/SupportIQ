# Product Warranty & Guarantee Policy (v2.0)

## Standard Warranty
All electronic accessories and gadgets sold on SupportIQ carry a standard **1-Year Manufacturer Warranty** covering mechanical and electrical defects.

## Warranty Exclusions
The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage caused by drops or liquid spills.
- Unauthorized repair attempts or modifications.
- Normal wear and tear over time.

## Filing a Warranty Claim
To file a claim, present your original Order ID to customer support. A support ticket will be opened to dispatch replacement hardware or issue a repair voucher.

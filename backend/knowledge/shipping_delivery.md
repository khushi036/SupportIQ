# Shipping & Delivery Policy (v2.4)

## Delivery Timelines
- **Standard Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days within India.
- **Express Delivery**: 1 to 2 business days for select metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru).
- **Same-Day Delivery**: Orders placed before 11:00 AM IST are eligible for same-day delivery by 8:00 PM IST.

## Order Tracking
Customers receive a tracking number via SMS and Email once the shipment leaves our warehouse. You can check your real-time order status by providing your 5-digit Order ID to our support agent or on our website.

## Delayed Shipments
If your order is out for delivery beyond the estimated date, our support team will automatically raise an inquiry with carrier partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, FedEx).
